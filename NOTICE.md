# Source material and rights

The MIT licence applies to the release code in `scripts/`, `workflow/` and `tests/`. Original command records and contributor scripts retain their authorship; their conversion into this directory structure does not transfer authorship.

The deposited sequence records and NCBI comparison export retain their source terms. Original microscopy, experimental plots, plasmid artwork and other supplied figures remain attributed to the study contributors. The software licence is not a claim to relicense third-party records or images. Publication of the included processed data, deidentified summaries, environmental coordinates and contributor artwork was authorised by the study principal investigator on 21 September 2026; their existing source terms and attribution remain in force.

The release preserves analytical identifiers but does not publish clinical linkage keys, patient-level free text, raw personal dates or private correspondence. Source-inventory paths refer to files within this archive or original basenames rather than private server locations, except where a historical command needs its original path for interpretation.
